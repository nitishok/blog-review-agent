# CEO Review Style

## Voice & Tone
- Direct and authoritative — no hedging language ("might", "could potentially", "it seems")
- Confident assertions, not suggestions
- Executive audience — assumes business literacy, not technical literacy

## Structural Preferences
- Lead with business value, not technology
- Problem → Solution → Outcome structure
- Short paragraphs — maximum 3 sentences
- Active voice throughout

## Appian Domain Rules
- Always tie claims to specific Appian capabilities (process automation, case management, data fabric, AI skills)
- Avoid generic "digital transformation" language without grounding in Appian outcomes
- Reference customer ROI metrics when possible (time saved, cost reduced, cycle time)

## Phrases to Avoid
- "leverage" → use "use" or "apply"
- "utilize" → use "use"
- "in order to" → use "to"
- "synergy", "paradigm shift", "holistic approach"
- Passive constructions like "it was found that", "results were seen"

## Phrases CEO Prefers
- "accelerate" (for speed outcomes)
- "streamline" (for process efficiency)
- "measurable results"
- Specific numbers over vague claims

## Call-to-Action Style
- Clear, single CTA per piece
- Action-oriented: "See how [Company] did it" not "Learn more"
