# Smarter Site Selection in Clinical Trials: where Process Automation meets AI

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

## A New Era for Feasibility in Clinical Research

In pharmaceutical R&D, selecting appropriate clinical trial sites presents both critical challenges and opportunities. Selecting the right site can accelerate enrollment, while poor choices delay timelines and increase expenses. Yet feasibility assessments remain largely manual, inconsistent, and fragmented across disconnected systems.

Process automation addresses this gap by unifying systems, standardizing data, and providing real-time insights for smarter site evaluation.

## Rethinking How We Approach Feasibility

Life sciences organizations face mounting pressures to advance protocol designs, adapt to regulatory changes, accelerate time-to-market, identify high-performing sites, and reach diverse patient populations. However, feasibility and site selection typically remain disconnected from real-time data, reliant on siloed systems, and dependent on manual workflows.

Automation enables organizations to keep pace with these demands while shifting from reactive to proactive decision-making.

## Why Automation Is a Game-Changer for Feasibility Teams

Process automation platforms unify disparate data sources through "Data Fabric"—a smart integration layer connecting modern cloud applications with legacy systems. This unified approach delivers:

- Customizable dashboards displaying site performance and regulatory readiness
- Visual workflows mapping to team processes
- Built-in collaboration tools for sponsors, CROs, and investigators

## High-Quality Data, Right from the Start

Process automation ensures data integrity through detecting inconsistencies, applying standardized terminologies, flagging quality issues, and enforcing governance policies across systems.

## What Unified Data Actually Delivers

Unified data views consolidate relevant information from multiple sources into single dashboards displaying site performance, patient demographics, and compliance data. Benefits include faster information access, comprehensive assessments, improved decision-making, reduced costs, better collaboration, and increased agility.

## Benefits for Every Stakeholder

**Investigators** gain faster access to comprehensive data and improved feasibility assessment capability. **Sponsors** achieve more accurate site selection and better trial oversight. **CROs** streamline multi-trial management. **Regulatory teams** access standardized data more easily and support faster submissions.

## Driving Smarter Decisions, Faster

Beyond unified systems, AI layered onto automation enables predictive capabilities. Rather than reviewing historical performance, AI can analyze vast datasets to predict which sites will deliver results for specific trials, flagging underperforming sites despite strong credentials, highlighting diverse patient access opportunities, and surfacing subtle risks through natural language processing of investigator notes.
