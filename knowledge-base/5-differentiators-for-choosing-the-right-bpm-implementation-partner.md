# 5 Differentiators for Choosing the Right BPM Implementation Partner

*By Amol Mategaonkar*

## Introduction

The author opens with an anecdote about discovering a new coffee shop that succeeded through unique differentiators—quality music, attention to detail, and excellent service—rather than just basic offerings. He draws a parallel to selecting Business Process Management (BPM) implementation partners, arguing that companies should look beyond standard capabilities.

## The Core Argument

While all competent BPM consulting firms provide "strong technical skills, project management experience, availability of needed resources at the right time, and competitive pricing," organizations should seek partners offering distinctive advantages that signal a committed long-term relationship.

## Five Key Differentiators

### 1. Flexible Engagement Model

Organizations need partners who adapt to their evolving needs rather than imposing rigid structures. Look for providers offering global delivery models with customizable mixes of onsite and offshore resources based on your preferences, not their resource constraints.

### 2. Thought Leadership

Seek partners demonstrating deep BPM expertise through community contributions, conference presentations, white papers, and industry authority. Companies hosting customer conferences and promoting best practice sharing demonstrate genuine commitment to advancing the field beyond standard project delivery.

### 3. Intellectual Property and Research Labs

Partners should offer innovation labs for testing emerging technologies, pre-built solutions demonstrating domain expertise, and Centers of Excellence providing ongoing technical leadership and team development.

### 4. Recognition from All Stakeholders

Evaluate vendors through multiple lenses: customer references and advocacy, BPM platform vendor partnerships, and analyst recognition. Industry analysts provide objective assessments across maturity, capacity, thought leadership, and innovation dimensions.

### 5. Trusted Partnership Through Transformation

Assess whether partners demonstrate genuine commitment to your long-term success through proactive solution suggestions, comprehensive support spanning strategy through analytics, and willingness to incorporate lessons learned.

## Conclusion

Selecting BPM implementation partners based on these differentiators ensures more rewarding, transformative long-term partnerships beyond immediate project needs.
