# BPM and Low-code – Synergies for Success

*By Santosh Subramanian*

Business Process Management (BPM) and Low-code application development represent two complementary technologies that have evolved to offer overlapping features and distinct capabilities. While some vendors combine both in a single platform, understanding how each technology works independently and together is essential for organizations seeking digital transformation.

## The Case for BPM

BPM transforms business processes by making them "efficient, agile, measurable, transparent, and scalable." The discipline follows a Model, Execute, Measure, and Improve methodology that has enabled business leaders since the late 1990s to gain unprecedented control over their operations.

Robust BPM platforms have delivered substantial business value through reduced operational costs, enhanced customer experiences, and improved efficiency. These systems equip leaders with monitoring, evaluation, and maintenance capabilities. In competitive markets, organizations increasingly target full process automation to minimize expenses.

## The Case for Low-code

Low-code enables rapid application development with minimal hand-coding through visual and declarative techniques. These platforms deliver modern, responsive applications with rich user interfaces at lower costs and faster deployment times than traditional development approaches.

The technology empowers business leaders to innovate quickly while focusing on customer experience rather than development complexity. Applications built on low-code platforms are mobile-enabled and require less time for building, testing, and deployment.

## The Synergy Between BPM and Low-code

"Low-code platforms are not really a replacement for BPM, but a powerful enhancement." While low-code accelerates app development, truly superior customer experiences require underlying business processes that are efficient, predictable, measurable, and mature—precisely what BPM delivers.

Modern BPM platforms increasingly incorporate low-code features, enabling process modeling, business rules application, and complex event adaptation. This combination produces applications that are both functionally robust and user-friendly, with mobile accessibility integrated throughout development.

## Conclusion

By leveraging both BPM and low-code principles together, organizations can build applications quickly with minimal resources while ensuring they rest upon strong business processes. This combination delivers competitive advantage through rapid innovation backed by operational excellence.
