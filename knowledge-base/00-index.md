# Princeton Blue Blog Knowledge Base — Index

## Executive Summary

Princeton Blue is an Appian-focused IT services and consulting firm that has built its content marketing entirely around life sciences process automation — specifically helping pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies modernize clinical, regulatory, and lab workflows using low-code platforms and, more recently, Agentic AI. Their marketing approach is practitioner-led (almost all content is authored by the founder or senior practitioners), deeply vertical-specific, and relentlessly focused on the business value of replacing manual/spreadsheet-driven processes with connected, auditable, AI-augmented workflows — positioning Princeton Blue as the indispensable implementation partner for this transformation.
