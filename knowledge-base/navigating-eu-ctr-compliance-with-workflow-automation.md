# Shortcast Episode 4: Navigating EU-CTR Compliance with Workflow Automation

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

The European Union Clinical Trial Regulation (EU-CTR) was established to bring standardization and openness to how trials are evaluated across Europe. For trial sponsors, however, keeping pace with these evolving requirements can seem daunting, particularly when depending on legacy, manual approaches.

In this episode, Pramod Sachdeva examines why EU-CTR compliance represents more than simply fulfilling a regulatory requirement, and how progressive organizations are reimagining their approach. He addresses the dangers of maintaining disconnected processes and utilizing basic collaboration tools like Excel, SharePoint, and email—which obscure process visibility and accountability. He explores how intelligent automation can enable a more coordinated, transparent submission workflow.

The discussion balances the complexity of the challenge with the potential of modernization, presented in accessible language without technical jargon. For organizations with EU-CTR on their priority list, this episode provides actionable perspective on what modernization can achieve.
