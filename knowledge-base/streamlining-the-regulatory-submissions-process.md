# Smarter Regulatory Submissions with Process Automation and AI

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

Life sciences companies operate in one of the most heavily regulated industries due to its direct impact on human health. Organizations must navigate complex compliance requirements throughout a drug's entire lifecycle—from development through approval and manufacturing to safety monitoring.

The regulatory submissions process forms a critical foundation for ensuring pharmaceutical products meet stringent safety, efficacy, and quality standards. However, many organizations still depend on outdated, manual workflows to manage this essential function. As data volumes and regulatory complexity grow, companies face mounting challenges in maintaining compliance while remaining competitive.

## Inside Today's Regulatory Submissions Process

### Why Manual Tasks Create Bottlenecks

The typical regulatory submissions workflow involves numerous interconnected activities: data entry, document management, cross-functional collaboration, and multi-level reviews. Most organizations still execute these tasks manually across disconnected systems.

Global teams frequently work with fragmented technology environments, relying on email and shared drives for document updates. This fragmentation introduces several problems: duplicate efforts, version control mismatches, unclear update status, and extended approval cycles. As submission volume increases, these inefficiencies make regulatory compliance and timeline adherence increasingly difficult with legacy technology infrastructure.

### How Workflow Automation Transforms Submissions

A typical regulatory submissions process involves multiple stakeholders performing interdependent tasks within structured business rule frameworks. The process requires multiple review and approval layers, emphasizing quality assurance and governance throughout. Maintaining strict GxP compliance and comprehensive audit trails with full traceability and accountability is essential.

## What Automation Can Do for Regulatory Submissions

Process automation platforms combined with AI capabilities enhance the complete submission lifecycle across six key areas:

**Document Identification and Collection**: Automation locates required documents from various systems using configurable business rules. AI employs Natural Language Processing to extract summaries and relevant sections from unstructured content like study reports and correspondence.

**Document Review and Approval**: Workflows direct documents to appropriate regulatory, clinical, and quality assurance stakeholders with built-in approval hierarchies and audit trails. AI functions as an additional reviewer, identifying data anomalies, flagging inconsistent terminology, and suggesting regulatory language adjustments aligned with specific agency requirements.

**Submission Package Assembly**: Automation compiles submission-ready packages using only the latest approved versions, minimizing manual effort and ensuring consistency. AI identifies relevant templates and content based on historical submissions, reducing repetitive work.

**Regulatory Compliance Checks**: Automated workflows verify submissions meet specific health authority requirements, checking formatting, completeness, and country-specific needs. AI scans against evolving global regulations from the FDA, EMA, and PMDA, alerting teams to requirement changes.

**Real-Time Status Tracking**: Managers access dashboards showing progress, bottlenecks, and ownership in real time. These dashboards can be proactive, identifying at-risk tasks before delays occur. AI provides predictive insights about sections likely causing delays or submissions facing higher rejection risk based on historical patterns.

**Audit Trail and Governance**: Every action is automatically logged, providing complete traceability for regulatory audits and authority queries.

## Why Automation Matters for Regulatory Submissions

Process automation delivers measurable business value across regulatory operations:

**Faster Time-to-Submission**: Automation accelerates document tracking, review cycles, and final packaging, allowing regulatory teams to move submissions through approval hierarchies more quickly without manual status checking. Clinical and safety data flows directly from systems like CTMS and EDC.

**Higher Submission Quality**: Automated compliance checks ensure completeness, structure, and formatting alignment with regulatory expectations, reducing rework and rejection risks.

**Greater Operational Efficiency**: Teams reuse approved content and templates instead of building submissions from scratch. This frees capacity for strategic work, allowing clinical, regulatory, and quality teams to focus on high-value activities rather than administrative tasks.

**Real-Time Visibility and Control**: Dashboards provide live views of submission progress across global teams without requiring manual follow-ups. AI enriches this visibility with delay forecasts and risk assessments.

**Enhanced Cross-Functional Collaboration**: A unified platform keeps regulatory affairs, clinical research, safety, and quality assurance teams aligned throughout the submission lifecycle, ensuring only the latest approved versions appear in final packages.

**Improved Regulatory Readiness**: Automation ensures consistency, traceability, and governance, enabling quick response to health authority queries with confidence in data integrity and audit trails.
