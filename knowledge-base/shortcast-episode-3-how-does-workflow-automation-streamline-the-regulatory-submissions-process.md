# Shortcast Episode 3: How does Process Automation Streamline Regulatory Submissions?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

Regulatory submissions represent one of the most intricate and time-sensitive phases of pharmaceutical development. These submissions span multiple nations and regulatory bodies, frequently requiring large teams distributed across locations to collaborate using outdated technologies like email, Excel, and SharePoint. This reliance on legacy tools creates significant challenges: poor visibility across teams, fragmented data storage, and elevated risks of submission delays that ultimately slow patient access to new therapies.

In this episode, Pramod Sachdeva, Founder & Managing Director at Princeton Blue, discusses how process automation is reshaping regulatory submissions work. He identifies key pain points when organizations depend on disconnected legacy systems, emphasizing how transparency gaps prevent regulatory teams from making informed decisions quickly.

The episode explores how modern automation solutions offer meaningful improvements. Real-time monitoring capabilities enable teams to track progress continuously. Streamlined document management reduces time spent searching across systems. Greater system flexibility allows organizations to adapt as submission requirements evolve.

Sachdeva underscores the value for pharmaceutical companies modernizing their regulatory operations, highlighting how workflow automation enhances operational efficiency, strengthens regulatory compliance, and accelerates the timeline for bringing new treatments to market.
