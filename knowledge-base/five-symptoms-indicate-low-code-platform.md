# Five Symptoms That Indicate You Need a Low-code Platform

*By Amol Mategaonkar*

Ignoring business symptoms can be costly in terms of cost overrun or missed opportunities. Here are five symptoms that indicate your organization needs to consider a Low-code platform for application development.

## Introduction

Picture a doctor addressing health problems: she listens to your symptoms, diagnoses the issue, and prescribes medicine. This reactive approach works, but what if problems are ignored until they become serious? The cost of delayed diagnosis can be enormous.

Similarly, organizations should proactively monitor business health for warning signs. Can your IT accelerate application development? Are you spending months programming instead of building applications in weeks? Can business users quickly create small applications with minimal IT involvement?

These scenarios call for a new paradigm: "low-code application development platform. A breakthrough in rapid application development and delivery with minimal coding." Traditional development processes struggle to deliver innovation quickly in today's digital landscape, potentially resulting in missed business opportunities.

## The Five Symptoms

### 1. Sluggish Pace of Innovation

Continuous innovation requires building, testing, and improving applications through multiple iterations. If you're constantly stuck in design-test-debug cycles, explore low-code development. These platforms offer "visual modeling, declarative programming and drag-and-drop components" enabling rapid development, configuration, testing, and release with minimal coding.

### 2. Lack of Agility in the IT Stack

Modern markets demand swift responses to changing business needs, market shifts, and regulatory changes. Legacy technology stacks cannot respond rapidly enough. Low-code platforms with configuration-based approaches and visual modeling tools help build agile applications that adapt to business changes quickly.

### 3. Inferior Customer Experience

Experience-driven customers instantly move to alternatives if applications fall short. Low-code platforms enable quick development, debugging, and deployment of mobile-enabled applications with rich user experiences, allowing fast iteration and upgrades.

### 4. Struggle with Mobile Enablement

Companies grappling with mobile enablement of legacy applications can benefit from low-code solutions. These platforms offer shorter development processes, on-the-fly testing, no required infrastructure, better data management, and flexibility in delivering quality customer experiences across devices and platforms.

### 5. Capex Budget Constraints

A major advantage of low-code platforms is the "cloud-based subscription model. Unlike legacy on-premise licensed technologies, cloud-based Low-code platforms need no upfront investment, and no associated infrastructure setup." This converts capital expenses to operational expenses, accelerating budget approval and streamlining future upgrades.

## Conclusion

Organizations should identify these symptoms early and convert challenges into opportunities. "Low-code is about getting more done quickly. Not just in less time, but also with less cost." Enterprise teams increasingly adopt low-code platforms to meet digital business demands for faster software delivery.
