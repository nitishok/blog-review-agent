# Shortcast Episode 8: How are leading pharma companies modernizing their labs?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

Pharma laboratories face mounting pressures to increase output while managing escalating sample volumes and intricate experiments. Traditional Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS), though historically foundational to lab operations, often impose rigid workflows and create data silos that impede efficiency.

## The Challenge

Contemporary pharma labs contend with several obstacles: expanding sample throughput, sophisticated experimental requirements, and stringent compliance mandates. "Pharma labs are under constant pressure to do more—faster," yet their legacy systems frequently become bottlenecks rather than enablers of progress.

## The Solution: Process Automation

Rather than pursuing expensive system replacements or extensive customizations, leading pharmaceutical organizations are discovering how process automation can modernize existing infrastructures. This approach delivers several advantages:

- **Real-time data circulation** across disconnected systems
- **Flexible, responsive workflows** that adapt to operational needs
- **Enhanced transparency** throughout laboratory processes
- **Strengthened compliance** mechanisms and documentation
- **Accelerated cycle times** without system overhauls

## Modern Lab Operations

When process automation works alongside LIMS, laboratories can achieve genuine agility. This partnership enables R&D and lab management teams to enhance operational capabilities while preserving existing technology investments, creating an environment where innovation and compliance coexist seamlessly.
