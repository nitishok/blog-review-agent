# Shortcast Episode 2: When Should Pharma Companies Start Looking at Workflow Automation?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

## Overview

This episode explores clinical supply chain management challenges and how process automation addresses them. The discussion centers on the difficulties pharmaceutical organizations face when managing complex operations.

## Key Content

Many pharma companies still depend on "a patchwork of legacy systems, Excel, SharePoint, and email to coordinate activities and transfer data." This fragmented approach creates significant operational risks, including missed releases, trial delays, and potential patient impact.

The episode identifies several core problems:

**Visibility and Integration Issues**: Organizations lack centralized oversight when systems operate in isolation. Without a unified source of truth, teams cannot easily track status across workflows, leading to inefficiencies and preventable errors.

**Process Complexity**: Manual coordination across disconnected tools creates bottlenecks and accountability gaps. Tasks may slip through without proper tracking or completion assurance.

## The Solution

Process automation orchestrates data integration across systems while streamlining collaboration. This approach enables:

- Transparent task assignment and tracking
- Enhanced visibility across the supply chain
- Standardized workflows with clear accountability
- Improved quality in review and release processes

The speaker emphasizes that automation doesn't just improve efficiency—it fundamentally strengthens one of life sciences' most critical operational areas.
