# CTA Patterns

## Overall CTA Philosophy: Soft Ask, Always

Princeton Blue consistently uses **low-commitment CTAs** — invitations to explore, contact, watch a demo, or read related content. There are no urgency-driven CTAs ("Limited time offer," "Book now"), no hard sells, and no gating of content behind lead capture forms (within the blog posts themselves). This is consistent with their consultative, relationship-first positioning.

---

## CTA Type Breakdown

### 1. "Contact Us" / "Talk to Us" — most common (~10 posts)
The most frequent CTA is a soft invitation to reach out. Variations:
- "Talk to us about solutions for these challenges." (post 3)
- "Send us a message" (post 18)
- "Talk to us" (BPM/Low-code synergies post)
- "See how we can help you" (post 30)
- Contact details: +1 (908) 369-0961 and [email protected] appear in several posts (posts 8, 9)

These CTAs are **passive** — they ask the reader to take action without giving them a reason to do so right now. No incentive, no urgency trigger, no specific next step framed as valuable.

### 2. "Watch the Demo" / "View the Solution Demo" — frequent (~8 posts)
Solution-specific demo CTAs are the most specific and action-oriented in the corpus:
- "Watch the Labeling Solution Demo" (post 24)
- "Watch the demo" — Study Data Access Management Solution (post 25)
- "Watch the Merck SCOPE 2023 presentation video" (post 26)
- Links to demo pages for Clinical Supplies Shipping, Labeling, Lab Experiment Automation, Lab Inventory Management

These are stronger CTAs because they offer something tangible (a demo video) rather than just "contact us." However, they're not consistently applied — many posts close without a demo link even when a relevant solution exists.

### 3. Related Resource Links — common in older posts
Several posts close by linking to 2-4 related blog posts rather than a commercial next step:
- "Explore related resources: 'Using a Modern BPM...', 'The Combined Power of RPA and BPM...'" (post 27)
- This pattern keeps readers on the site but misses the opportunity to push toward a sales conversation

### 4. "Explore Our Solution" / "Learn More" — occasional
A few posts link to specific solution pages on the Princeton Blue website:
- "Explore Princeton Blue's Regulatory Submissions solution" (post 7) — the most assertive commercial CTA in the corpus
- "Brainstorm with our team of experts and use our Innovation Lab to see what a potential solution could look like" (post 15) — this is distinctive, positions a proof-of-concept conversation as the CTA

### 5. "Schedule a Consultation" — rare but strongest
Post 5 ends with: "Schedule a consultation to explore how Process Automation and Agentic AI can address your specific supply chain challenges." This is the clearest, most conversion-oriented CTA in all 30 posts — it names the form of engagement (consultation), the topic (supply chain), and the value prop (address your specific challenges). Used in only 1 post.

### 6. eBook / Content Offer — appears once
Post 30 ("Get High on Low-code") offers a downloadable eBook: "Discover Low-code Application Development." This is the only gated or downloadable content CTA across all 30 posts — a significant gap in lead generation infrastructure.

## CTA Placement

- Consistently placed at the **end of the post** (post-conclusion)
- Rarely appears mid-post (one exception: post 7 links to a solution page inline within the body)
- The Shortcast episode posts end with a brief "listen/explore" CTA that doubles as the episode description

---

## CTA Consistency Issues

1. **No consistent CTA across all posts** — each post has a slightly different CTA or no CTA at all
2. **No primary conversion goal is evident** — is the goal to book a call? Watch a demo? Download a resource? The inconsistency suggests the CTAs were written post-hoc rather than from a defined funnel strategy
3. **Author bio replaces CTA in some posts** — several posts end with a detailed author bio (Pramod Sachdeva credentials) without a commercial CTA, which is a missed opportunity
4. **No urgency or social proof at CTA point** — "Talk to us" without a reason to do so today has weak conversion potential

---

## Recommended CTA Improvements

- Standardize a primary CTA (e.g., "Schedule a 30-minute process review with our team")
- Add social proof at CTA point: "Join 50+ pharma companies who have modernized with Princeton Blue"
- Use more demo CTAs: demos are the strongest CTA in the corpus
- Consider mid-post CTAs for long-form posts
- Add one gated asset per major topic cluster (regulatory submissions, clinical ops, lab) to generate leads
