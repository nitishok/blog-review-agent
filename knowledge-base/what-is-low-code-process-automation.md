# Shortcast Episode 6: What is Low-code Process Automation?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

As pharmaceutical companies face accelerating drug discovery timelines, those that succeed will be able to respond swiftly to market shifts and regulatory changes. Traditional siloed systems often hinder this agility, making low-code process automation an increasingly vital capability across life sciences.

In this episode, Pramod Sachdeva, Founder and Managing Director of Princeton Blue, discusses how low-code automation platforms are reshaping pharmaceutical operations. Rather than replacing existing infrastructure, these solutions integrate with systems like Veeva, Argus, SAP, and Oracle to consolidate data and workflows into unified, automated processes.

Sachdeva emphasizes that low-code process automation has become essential for clinical, regulatory, laboratory, and manufacturing departments. Drawing on experience delivering hundreds of automation initiatives for major pharmaceutical organizations, he highlights how companies achieve shortened cycle times, enhanced cross-functional collaboration, and regulatory compliance while reaching implementation value in weeks rather than years.

The discussion underscores how low-code automation is fundamentally transforming pharmaceutical operations, enabling teams to operate more efficiently and confidently at accelerated speeds.
