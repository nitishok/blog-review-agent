# Is Process Automation the Future of the Pharma Regulatory Labeling Process?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

## Introduction

Pharmaceutical label modifications occur for various reasons, including dosage adjustments, formulation updates, and new safety data. The regulatory labeling change process demands strict compliance with country-specific regulations and involves intricate workflows spanning globally distributed teams. Manual execution of these changes creates delays, errors, and unnecessary expenses.

## The Current State of the Regulatory Labeling Process

Label change completion typically requires several months to over a year, involving extensive documentation, regulatory approvals, and stakeholder communication. Many organizations execute these processes through siloed country-specific systems, preventing unified monitoring and proactive delay identification.

## Business Drivers for Managing Label Changes

Key imperatives include:

- Maintaining accurate, current label information
- Adhering to strict regulatory compliance requirements
- Completing changes promptly
- Ensuring effective stakeholder alignment and communication
- Providing appropriate translation and localization
- Maintaining consistency across all product labels and formats
- Closely monitoring status across all countries
- Proactively identifying and minimizing delays

## The Modern Approach: Low-Code Process Automation

Low-code automation represents a relevant solution, enabling "rapid application creation using drag-and-drop interfaces and pre-built components" with minimal coding requirements. This technology integrates real-time data from existing systems, automates decision-making through configurable rules, provides interactive dashboards, and delivers modern user experiences while improving efficiency and reducing compliance risks.

## Case Study Results

A Fortune 100 pharmaceutical company implementing this approach achieved:

- **50% cycle time reduction**: Cut new drug label approval from approximately 12 months to under 6 months
- **Centralized global operations**: Configured country-specific regulatory workflows with transparent tracking
- **Reduced errors**: Built-in form validation improved accuracy
- **Unified data access**: Gained comprehensive insights into global label change processes

## Eight-Step Implementation Approach

1. Assess current systems, stakeholders, and business drivers
2. Map existing processes identifying manual steps and redundancies
3. Define automation requirements including workflows and integrations
4. Select appropriate low-code automation platform
5. Design data models, workflows, and business rules
6. Develop and implement solution components
7. Test thoroughly and deploy for team usage
8. Continuously monitor and optimize performance

## Benefits of Automation

Implementing process automation delivers:

- Reduced time and resource requirements for compliance management
- Improved accuracy and consistency in label changes
- Consistent process execution with built-in audit trails
- Elimination of manual data entry and email approvals
- Enhanced stakeholder collaboration and communication
- Real-time transparency enabling remote monitoring across devices
