# Shortcast Episode 5: How is RPA Different from Workflow Automation?

*By Pramod Sachdeva*

## Overview

This episode explores the distinctions between Robotic Process Automation (RPA) and workflow automation in life sciences operations. According to Pramod Sachdeva, Founder & Managing Director of Princeton Blue, the two approaches serve different but complementary purposes.

## Key Differences

**RPA's Role**: RPA functions as "a digital workforce" handling repetitive, rules-based tasks with speed and precision. These bots excel at executing the same actions consistently without variation.

**Workflow Automation's Purpose**: Workflow automation orchestrates broader organizational processes, facilitating seamless collaboration across people, systems, and departments. It manages the bigger strategic picture rather than individual tactical tasks.

## Synergistic Relationship

Rather than competing technologies, RPA and workflow automation create what Sachdeva describes as "a match made in automation heaven." In practice, bots handle tasks where human intervention would slow processes, while humans guide the exceptions and complex decisions that exceed automation capabilities.

## Business Impact

This combined approach reshapes how organizations approach efficiency and compliance. As companies embrace digital transformation, this synergy between RPA and workflow automation increasingly becomes central to modernizing operations across clinical, regulatory, and laboratory functions.

The shortcast provides practical clarity on where each technology excels and how they integrate within real-world life sciences solutions.
